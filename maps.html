Each island is a point subsample of one of our published maps (the stereo depth of xclean's keyframes, placed with xclean's live poses), with xclean's live keyframe route in blue. Island sizes and positions are an arrangement, not to scale. Rosario islands: The Rosario Dataset, CIFASIS-CONICET, CC BY 4.0. Only maps from data we may share are published here: our own OAK-D-Lite recordings and the Rosario Dataset (CC BY 4.0). Routes from datasets with non-commercial or unstated licences appear on the results page as numbers only. The poses of each map are the median run of the three final-board runs (by ATE on routes with ground truth; on our OAK routes, the run whose end position is closest to the other two). Map facts are read from each map's own files (attribution, manifest, trajectory, point cloud); path length is the sum of xclean's keyframe-to-keyframe distances. The board numbers shown come from an engine frozen with its last pre-registered kill test (K14) failed on two routes; details.
