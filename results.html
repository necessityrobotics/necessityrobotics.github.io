Disclosures

Read this first

  • Most of our pre-registered claims did not pass. On the 38 unseen routes, 1 of 12 claims passed. The full list.

Definitions

Unseen route
A route xclean had never been run on before the final board. 22 were never run; 16 more were sealed, their data never opened. Seen routes were used while building xclean or by earlier kill tests.
Lost run
A run in which more than 10 % of the frames have no published pose, or a state other than tracking, relocalised or initialising, or which ends in an unrecovered loss.
Continuity
Every correction of the published map pose is flagged, and the odometry stream is never corrected (claim C2).
Unsupported frames
Frames with no published pose, a state other than tracking, or a two-second motion that disagrees with ground truth, as a share of the run. The bar is 2 % per route.
ATE
Absolute trajectory error in metres, at keyframe level, on the time window both systems cover, after rigid alignment to ground truth.
Latency
Recorder receipt of the loop-corrected pose minus the release of its frame, over every frame; a dropped frame counts as infinitely late.
