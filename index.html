01 · Who we are

A perception company.

Necessity Robotics works on perception: how a robot works out where it is and what is around it, from its own cameras, live, on its own computer.

We build in the open. We measure on public benchmarks and on our own recordings, we fix our rules before the runs, and we publish every result, including the losses.

  • xclean: real-time stereo visual-inertial SLAM, open source.
  • Maps: 3-D Gaussian-splat maps built from xclean's own live camera poses.
  • A technical paper on the system and on how we score it live.

02 · xclean

Live stereo visual-inertial SLAM.

xclean turns a stereo camera and its IMU into a camera pose for every frame, while the robot moves, with loop closure, relocalisation and map reuse. Its GPU code is written in CuPy: no C++ build step, no ROS, no learned networks in the SLAM pipeline, native on Windows 11.

Sensors

Stereo + IMU
