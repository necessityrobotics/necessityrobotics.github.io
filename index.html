Why black and white?

The 5 places from India are black and white, because our camera's two eyes see no colour. The 3 field places from Argentina are in colour.

Why does it look soft?

A computer built it from the photos. No person touched it up.

Where it is not perfect

Depth model · nrs-large

Zero-shot stereo depth.

Two cameras, like two eyes. This ML model (machine learning, a kind of AI) works out how far away each point is. It learned only from computer-made images. We tested it on real photos from cameras it never saw. Our 3-D places take their depth from it.

  • From scratch. 155,769 synthetic photo pairs, 200,000 training steps, one graphics card.
  • 57 million parameters. The weights are ours. The design, DLNR, is from another team.
  • Zero-shot. Tested on real photos it never saw. Pass marks set before training: 4 of 4 passed.
  • Any calibrated stereo camera (pinhole lenses). Zero-shot: no retraining for your camera. Free download.
  • Not for live use. 0.28 s per photo pair on one RTX 5090, so it gives depth to our map builder after the recording. xclean, our live SLAM, uses no neural network.

For engineers

The method, every result and the code.

xclean is real-time stereo visual-inertial SLAM. It gives a pose for every camera frame. The IMU is optional. Open source, Apache-2.0.

Every run of record is published: the losses, the flagged wins, and the pre-registered test (K14) it did not pass. We do not claim to be more accurate.
